# Changes and retests

## Documented prototype directions

- Dense surface flecks were simplified after the visual critique that the character looked crowded. Later art restored warmer toasted edges and broad highlights while keeping the face clear.
- Basket/plate collection and mash/ghost feedback make round outcomes visible.
- A persistent wallet and cosmetic shop address the team-identified weakness of round-only accumulation.
- Player-ranking and customization directions informed the optimized avatar, local Hall of Fame and same-screen modes.

These are design/implementation directions from supplied records, not a new user study. Their benefit must be validated with actual players.
