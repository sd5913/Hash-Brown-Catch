WELCOME TO THE CLUB

HOW TO PLAY &
PARTY TOGETHER.

  1. Pick 1P or Same-Screen 2P. Play 1P Solo, 2P VS PK (P1 A/D vs P2 ←/→ or mouse with basket-bump knockback!), or 2P Co-op (+5 bonus when P1 and P2 alternate catches).
  2. Generate your Pixel Potato Face. Open PIXEL FACE to upload any avatar/selfie (or snap via webcam) — our Bayer-dithered quantizer bakes your face right onto the hash brown!
  3. Beat the Final 5s Big Countdown. Watch the giant center-screen 5-4-3-2-1 countdown in the closing seconds and bank +2 permanent coins per golden catch for the 7-item cosmetic shop.
  4. Pause or Exit anytime. Press SPACE or P to pause, ESC to exit a round straight back to the menu, and R on results to replay.
  5. Climb the Hall of Fame. Save your nickname and custom pixel portrait onto the local arcade leaderboard after any round!

Golden catches earn +2 permanent coins for the Treat Shop. Misses and hazards never take wallet coins away. All data stays private in this browser.
